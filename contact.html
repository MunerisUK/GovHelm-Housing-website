Accessibility

Talk to us

What helps us give you a useful answer

GovHelm Housing is built and supported by Muneris. There is no reseller channel and no partner tier, so whoever you speak to can answer for the product as well as the commercials.

When you get in touch, it helps enormously to say which kind of organisation you are, roughly what scale, which part of the platform you are interested in, and when your incumbent contract ends. Two lines of context saves a fortnight of email.

Please do not send personal data about residents or case details in an enquiry. If a question requires it, we will agree a secure route first.

Response times

  • Security reports — acknowledged within two working days
  • Evaluation requests — usually the same working day
  • Pricing and general enquiries — within two working days
  • Customer support — to the targets in your support agreement, where you hold one

Contact details are not listed on this page yet. They go here before the site is published.

Before you call

Three things worth knowing, so the conversation starts in the right place.

  • GovHelm Housing is pre-release, working towards 1.0
  • It is commercial software with a licence fee, set deliberately low
  • The licence covers every module, with no per-seat count
  • Implementation and migration are real work, and quoted honestly
  • We will tell you early if GovHelm Housing is a poor fit for you
