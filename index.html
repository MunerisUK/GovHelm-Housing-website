Housing services, joined up from application to tenancy

GovHelm Housing is an integrated housing management platform: a single, modular system covering the housing register, allocations, lettings, homelessness casework and tenancy management, licensed at a price that does not punish you for using it.

The problem

Housing teams run on systems that were never designed to talk to each other

A household applying for housing can appear in six different systems with six different spellings of their name. Officers rekey data between them, residents repeat their story to every service, and returns are assembled by hand from exports that disagree with each other.

The cost is not only licensing. It is the fortnight spent reconciling a CORE return, the homelessness decision delayed because the medical assessment sits in another team's spreadsheet, and the resident who gives up part-way through an application form that forgot what they typed on the previous page.

GovHelm Housing takes the opposite position. One record for the household. One place where the allocation scheme is written down as rules. One API for everything else you run. And a commercial licence priced low enough that adopting it is a service decision rather than a capital programme.

What changes on day one

  • Applicants stop repeating themselves between services
  • Allocation decisions carry a written, auditable reason
  • Statutory returns are assembled from live case data
  • Officers work in one interface, not five
  • Your data stays in your estate, in documented formats
  • The cost of a change is the work, not the contract

Every claim on this page is something you can put to the test in an evaluation environment before you commit.

How it works

An opinionated core, an open edge

  1. 1

    Model the household once

    Applicants, tenants and household members live in a single party record with a full relationship and address history. Every module reads from it.

  2. 2

    Write the scheme down as rules

    Your allocation scheme, banding criteria and local lettings plans are configuration, not code. Change them without a release.

  3. 3

    Let the workflow carry the statutory clock

    Timescales, review points and duty decisions are tracked by the platform, with alerts before a deadline rather than after.

  4. 4

    Publish the data you already hold

    Returns, dashboards and the resident portal all read the same case data. Nothing is assembled twice.

Built for the rules you actually work to

Statutory context is not an add-on

GovHelm Housing is written for English and Welsh housing legislation and the returns that follow from it. The obligations below shaped the data model rather than being bolted on afterwards.

Housing Act 1996, Parts VI & VII

Allocation schemes, reasonable preference and the full homelessness duty sequence are first-class concepts in the model.

Homelessness Reduction Act 2017

Prevention and relief duties, Personalised Housing Plans and the duty-to-refer inbox are part of casework, not a separate register.

CORE and H-CLIC returns

Return fields are captured during normal work and validated before submission, so the return is a report rather than a project.

UK GDPR and DPA 2018

Lawful basis, retention and subject access are enforced by the platform, with every read and write recorded.

WCAG 2.2 AA

The public sector accessibility duty is met by design, and tested with assistive technology on every release.

HACT UK Housing Data Standards

Shared vocabulary for property, person and tenancy so your data travels between systems without a translation layer.

Commercial, and deliberately low cost

Priced to be adopted

GovHelm Housing is commercial software with a licence fee. The fee is set low on purpose, because the barrier to better housing systems has rarely been the software itself.

Procurement in housing has produced a small number of large systems, long contracts and expensive change control. The licence is rarely the largest line in the business case; the change requests over the following five years are. A system that is cheap to buy and expensive to alter is not cheap.

So GovHelm Housing is priced to be adopted rather than endured, and built so that the things providers change most often — the allocation scheme, banding rules, letters and forms — are configuration you control rather than chargeable work.
