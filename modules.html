Platform

Twelve modules over one core

Every module shares the same household record, permission model and audit trail. Adopt them together or bring them in one at a time alongside what you already run.

Modules

The full set

Modules are deployed as separate services but configured as one platform. Turning a module off removes its screens and its API surface; it never leaves orphaned data behind.

Built or designed?

GovHelm Housing is pre-release, so each module below is marked Built if it is in a release you can run today, or Designed if it is specified and scheduled but not yet written. No module is yet running a live housing service.

See the full capability status

Housing register

Applications & registration

Built

One front door for every housing application. Online, assisted digital or officer-keyed, with the same validation rules behind each route.

  • Configurable application forms with save-and-return
  • Duplicate and fraud checks at the point of submission
  • Evidence upload with virus scanning and retention rules
  • Automatic acknowledgement and statutory timescale clocks

Assessment

Needs assessment & banding

Built

Encode your allocation scheme once. Every assessment then applies it consistently and shows the applicant exactly how their band was reached.

  • Rules engine for bands, points and priority dates
  • Medical and welfare assessment workflows
  • Full decision audit with reason codes
  • Scheme versioning so historic decisions stay explainable

Lettings

Allocations & shortlisting

Built

Shortlist against the scheme in seconds, with quotas, sensitive lets and local connection rules applied automatically.

  • Automated shortlisting with manual override and reason
  • Quota, sensitive let and local lettings plan support
  • Offer, refusal and reasonable preference tracking
  • Bypass and exception reporting for audit

Lettings

Choice-based lettings

Built

Publish adverts, take bids and run cycles on a schedule, with the applicant seeing their position in real time.

  • Advert scheduling with property attributes and photos
  • Bidding by web, phone or text with accessibility fallbacks
  • Cycle automation and results publication
  • Feedback data published back to applicants

Statutory duty

Homelessness & casework

Built · RC

Prevention, relief and main duty casework that follows the Homelessness Reduction Act without officers rekeying anything.

  • Personalised Housing Plans with task tracking
  • Duty decision letters generated from case data
  • Temporary accommodation placement and charging
  • H-CLIC fields captured as part of normal casework

Tenancy

Tenancy management

Designed

From sign-up to termination, with the tenancy record as the single source of truth for everyone who touches it.

  • Sign-up, succession, assignment and mutual exchange
  • Introductory and flexible tenancy reviews
  • Household composition and occupancy history
  • Tenancy audit visits and outcomes

Assets

Repairs & asset interface

Designed

A clean boundary to your repairs and asset systems so property data stays correct in both directions.

  • Property, block and scheme hierarchy
  • Repair raising and status write-back
  • Compliance flags surfaced on the tenancy record
  • Void turnaround tracking through to re-let

Income

Income, rents & arrears

Designed

Rent accounts, charges and arrears actions that stay aligned with the tenancy and the housing register.

  • Rent and service charge setting with annual uplift
  • Arrears escalation paths with pre-action protocol steps
  • Universal Credit managed payment handling
  • Affordability and income data captured once

Operations

Documents & correspondence

Built

Every letter, notice and form produced from templates you control, in plain English and accessible formats.

  • Template library with version control
  • Merge from live case data, no rekeying
  • Print, email, SMS and portal delivery
  • Full correspondence history on the case

Reporting

Statutory returns & reporting

Built · RC

Returns assembled from the data officers already captured, with validation before anything is submitted.

  • CORE lettings and sales return preparation
  • H-CLIC extract with pre-submission validation
  • Operational dashboards for team leaders
  • Warehouse-friendly exports for corporate BI

Residents

Citizen portal

Built

One account for applicants and tenants: apply, bid, report, pay and track, on any device, in any supported language.

  • WCAG 2.2 AA interface tested with assistive technology
  • Application, bid and case status in real time
  • Secure document upload and message threads
  • Multi-language content with translation workflow

Platform

Integrations hub

Built · RC

The connective tissue: a documented API, an event stream and adapters for the systems you already run.

  • REST API described by an OpenAPI 3.1 specification
  • Outbound webhooks and an internal event bus
  • Adapters for finance, CRM, document and asset systems
  • Scheduled import and reconciliation jobs

Adoption patterns

You do not have to take all of it at once

Most providers start with the modules that hurt most and extend outwards. These are the three routes we see.

Most common

Register first

Applications, assessment and allocations go live together, with lettings and the portal following in the next release. Existing tenancy and finance systems stay in place and are fed through the integrations hub.

Statutory pressure

Homelessness first

Casework, Personalised Housing Plans and H-CLIC go first where the homelessness service is under the most pressure, with the register following once the case data is trusted.

Replacement

Whole platform

A full replacement at the end of an incumbent contract, usually phased by service area over two or three releases with a parallel-run period.

Core is not optional

The core provides the party record, address and property references, scheme rules, workflow, permissions, audit and the event bus. Every module depends on it, and it is always deployed.
