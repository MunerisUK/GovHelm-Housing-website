Platform

Everything a housing service does, in one place

A tour of what GovHelm Housing actually does — for applicants and tenants, for the officers who serve them, and for the people who have to report on all of it.

Module detailRequest an evaluation

For residents

A service that remembers you

The resident-facing side of GovHelm Housing is the part most people will ever see. It is built to the same standard as anything else the public sector publishes.

Apply once

Save and return at any point. Evidence uploaded once is reused across the application, the assessment and any later homelessness case.

See the position honestly

Band, effective date, bid history and shortlist position, shown as they are rather than as a queue number that means nothing.

Bid without a phone queue

Choice-based lettings adverts, bidding and results in the browser, with telephone and text fallbacks for people who need them.

One thread per case

Secure messages to the officer handling the case, with every letter sent available in the same place.

Report and pay

Tenants raise repairs, check rent accounts and make payments against the same account they applied with.

In their own language

Multi-language content with a translation workflow, and interfaces tested with screen readers and magnification.

For officers

Fewer systems, fewer clicks, better decisions

The staff workspace is organised around the household rather than the module. An officer opening a record sees the application, the assessment, any live homelessness case, the tenancy history and the correspondence trail together, subject to their permissions.

Work is driven by queues that respect statutory clocks. A relief duty approaching day 56 surfaces before it expires, not in next month's exception report. Decisions are made in the flow of work, with the scheme rules and the evidence on the same screen.

Bulk operations are treated as first-class: shortlist a cycle, issue a review batch, apply an annual rent uplift, each with a preview, an approval step and a reversible audit entry.

  • Household-centred record, not module-centred
  • Work queues driven by statutory timescales
  • Scheme rules visible at the point of decision
  • Evidence and correspondence on the same screen
  • Bulk actions with preview, approval and rollback
  • Keyboard-first navigation throughout
  • Role-based views that hide irrelevant clutter
  • Every override captured with a reason code
