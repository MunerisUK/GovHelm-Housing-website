Security and data protection

Housing systems hold some of the most sensitive personal data a public body processes: health, domestic abuse, immigration status, children. This page sets out how IHAP protects it and what remains your responsibility.

  • Defence in depth
  • UK GDPR by design
  • Independently reviewable source

Controls

What the platform does

Authentication

Staff authenticate through your identity provider over OIDC or SAML 2.0. IHAP holds no staff passwords. Resident accounts support multi-factor authentication and account recovery that does not rely on knowledge questions.

Authorisation

Role-based access resolved centrally and enforced twice: at the API boundary and again in the query layer. Field-level redaction applies to restricted case types and protected addresses.

Audit

Append-only logging of reads as well as writes, with actor, timestamp, correlation identifier and, where required, a stated reason for access. Exportable to your SIEM.

Encryption

TLS 1.3 in transit. Encryption at rest for the database and object store, with application-level encryption for the most sensitive fields.

Secrets and keys

No secrets in configuration files or images. Integration with your secret manager, with documented rotation procedures.

Supply chain

Signed container images, a software bill of materials issued with every release, dependency scanning in CI and a documented patch cadence.

Data protection

Designed around UK GDPR and the DPA 2018

Compliance is not a document you write afterwards. These are the parts the platform enforces rather than merely enables.

ObligationHow IHAP supports it
Lawful basis and purpose limitationEach case type declares its lawful basis and purpose. Data captured for one purpose is not silently reused for another, and cross-purpose access is logged.
Data minimisationForms and assessments only collect fields the configured scheme actually uses. Unused fields can be switched off rather than left blank.
AccuracyOne household record with a correction workflow, so fixing a date of birth fixes it everywhere rather than in one module.
Storage limitationRetention schedules per case type, evaluated automatically against closure dates, with legal hold and a reviewable deletion log.
Subject accessA single export covering every module, with redaction tooling for third-party data and a record of what was disclosed.
Rectification and erasureWorkflows that respect statutory retention: where erasure cannot be granted, the platform records the exemption relied on.
Special category dataHealth, ethnicity and similar data held in restricted fields with separate permissions and mandatory access reasons.
Records of processingProcessing activities generated from live configuration, so your ROPA reflects the system as deployed.
DPIA supportA template data protection impact assessment covering the platform's standard processing, for you to adapt.

Threat model

What we defend against, and what we do not claim

In scope

  • Unauthorised access to case data by an authenticated user outside their role
  • Enumeration of applicants or properties through the API or portal
  • Injection, deserialisation and file upload attacks
  • Session fixation and cross-site request forgery
  • Insider browsing of records without a service reason
  • Tampering with the audit trail
  • Exfiltration through export and reporting features

Your responsibility

  • Network boundary, WAF and DDoS protection
  • Identity provider configuration, including MFA for staff
  • Patching the hosts and orchestrator you run on
  • Backup, restore testing and disaster recovery
  • Role assignment and periodic access review
  • Monitoring the audit stream you export

Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities to the security contact rather than through general support, so they reach the response team directly.

We acknowledge reports within two working days, agree a disclosure timeline with the reporter, and credit reporters in the release notes unless they ask us not to. Researchers acting in good faith will not be pursued.

Security contact details

Assurance

The platform is built to support Cyber Essentials Plus certification and the NCSC Cloud Security Principles. Summaries of independent penetration testing are issued to licensed providers with each major release, along with a scope template for commissioning your own.

Certification applies to a deployment, not to a product. We supply the evidence; your organisation certifies its own instance.

What we will show you

Assurance without a source licence

IHAP is commercial software, so the source is not published. That should not stop your security team satisfying itself about the controls, and the following are available to providers evaluating or running the platform.

  • Independent penetration test summaries for each major release, including the findings and their remediation status.
  • A software bill of materials for every release, so you can run the dependency list through your own vulnerability process.
  • Source code review under non-disclosure, for the authentication path, the permission resolver and the audit implementation. Public sector security teams ask for this and we say yes.
  • Source code escrow, so a supplier failure does not leave you without the means to keep the system running.
  • Completed security questionnaires against the frameworks your assurance process uses, rather than a marketing datasheet.

What none of that changes is operational discipline. An unpatched IHAP is as vulnerable as an unpatched anything. The deployment guide covers patch cadence, and every release carries a security section in its notes.
